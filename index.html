FEATURED AI PROJECT

Herculex AI

An AI-powered response assistant built in Python that generates draft replies to show cause notices (SCNs). It parses notice content, extracts allegations and deadlines, and produces structured, professional drafts that legal and HR teams can review and approve.

Drafting SCN replies is repetitive and demands strict factual accuracy. Herculex AI automates the first draft by extracting key facts and applying configurable legal templates — reducing drafting time while keeping a qualified human in the loop before anything is sent.

View on GitHub

Core Stack

Python, FastAPI, LLMs

Input Processing

OCR, entity extraction

Output Style

Template-driven drafts

Compliance

Audit logs, verification flags

Context extraction

Identifies allegations, dates, parties, and requested actions from notice text and attachments — normalizing varied formats into structured facts for generation.

Template-driven drafts

Produces formal, review-ready replies using legal and HR templates — covering chronology, mitigation, and remedial actions with consistent tone across cases.

Safety & human review

Flags statements needing human confirmation, applies post-generation checks to reduce hallucinations, and maintains full audit logs for compliance and traceability.

FEATURED FULL-STACK PROJECT

Herculex Newsletter Agent

An end-to-end AI platform that automates weekly legal newsletter production for Indian indirect tax and trade policy — scraping GST, customs, excise, and trade policy updates from government portals, summarizing PDFs with specialized LLM agents, and rendering a branded HTML newsletter with inline editing and PDF export.

Built to replace a manual weekly workflow, the app supports two paths: upload PDFs per section and generate, or scrape official CBIC tax portals automatically, run parallel AI agents, and deliver a polished newsletter ready for legal review.

Frontend

React, Vite, Tailwind

Backend

FastAPI, Playwright

AI Pipeline

4 parallel LLM agents

Storage

Appwrite DB + files

Automated portal scraping

Playwright automates CBIC's Tax Information Portal — navigating dynamic tabs, category filters, date ranges, and PDF downloads across GST, customs, excise, and service tax sections.

Multi-agent summarization

Four specialized agents process sections in parallel with structured JSON schemas — classifying documents, extracting legal provisions, summarizing case law, and generating editorial copy.

Review & publish workflow

React UI with drag-and-drop uploads, async job polling, in-browser rich-text editing, and client-side PDF export — giving legal teams full control before publication.
